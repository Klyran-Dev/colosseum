# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and inclusive community.

Expected behavior:

- communicate respectfully and assume good intent;
- focus criticism on designs, code, evidence, and behavior rather than people;
- welcome questions and explain decisions clearly;
- protect private data, credentials, security reports, and sensitive tax evidence;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes promptly.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, abuse of security reports, and knowingly misleading claims about safety, tax coverage, performance, or production status.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action needed to protect the community.
